What's probably broken on your site right now

These are the six issues I find most often on small business websites — each one is quietly costing you visibility.

✕

No meta titles or descriptions

Google can't write a good search snippet for you, so it writes a bad one — or skips you entirely.

Common on 80% of sites I audit
✕

Slow page speed

Unoptimized images and bloated scripts push load time past 4 seconds — Google ranks slow sites lower.

Fixable in one pass
✕

Not mobile-optimized

Most local searches happen on a phone. If the site breaks on mobile, it drops in rankings immediately.

Rebuilt with Next.js
✕

No structured data

Without schema markup, Google can't show your business hours, reviews, or location in search results.
